# BTC R1 Homepage Refresh Design

## Goal

Refresh the public web page for patients and caregivers worried about cognitive impairment or dementia, while also supporting purchase conversion for cognitive learning materials and recruitment of cognitive learning instructors.

## Primary Conversion Strategy

Use a balanced strategy with the first viewport focused on products. The page should immediately communicate that visitors can buy practical cognitive learning workbooks, guidebooks, and kits for home or institutional use. The same page should also build trust by showing social contribution, professional credibility, instructor recruitment, and a clear link to the information blog at `https://neudoc.tistory.com/`.

## Audience

- Patients or caregivers looking for practical cognitive learning materials.
- Care centers, welfare centers, hospitals, and institutions that need structured cognitive programs.
- Prospective cognitive learning instructors who want education or partnership opportunities.

## Page Structure

1. Hero section
   - Message: cognitive learning materials for home and institution use.
   - Primary CTA: view materials or shop.
   - Secondary CTAs: instructor inquiry and blog.
   - Visual: product or learning scene imagery already available in the project.

2. Product highlights
   - Show the main learning sets, guidebooks, Q&A booklet, and behavior response booklet.
   - Emphasize practical use, level-based structure, and instructor guidance.

3. Who it helps
   - Caregivers and families.
   - Day care and senior care institutions.
   - Cognitive learning instructors and educators.

4. Social contribution and credibility
   - Position the company as contributing to dementia prevention, caregiver education, and community cognitive health.
   - Use calm, professional language without making medical diagnosis claims.

5. Instructor recruitment
   - Invite cognitive learning instructors.
   - Explain that instructors can use structured materials and guide programs in communities and institutions.

6. Blog connection
   - Link clearly to `https://neudoc.tistory.com/` for information and educational articles.

7. Final CTA
   - Encourage product purchase, consultation, and instructor inquiry.

## Visual Direction

Use a refined, trustworthy look with a white base, deep green/navy text, and warm accent colors. Avoid a cold hospital-like page and avoid a generic marketing landing page. The design should feel practical, social-impact oriented, and easy to scan on desktop and mobile.

## Responsive Behavior

The page must work well on desktop, tablet, and smartphone. On mobile, CTAs should appear early, product cards should stack cleanly, and the header menu should remain easy to use.

## Implementation Scope

Update the homepage and shared header/footer text where needed. Replace broken Korean text with clear Korean copy. Reuse existing local assets and product data where possible. Connect the internal blog navigation to the external Tistory blog URL.

## Non-Goals

Do not implement new checkout logic, payment integration, or a new content management system. Do not make medical diagnosis claims.
